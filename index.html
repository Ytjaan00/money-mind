⚠️ The Interest Trap — Financial Wisdom

💀 Interest is the silent wealth destroyer. It takes from the poor and gives to the rich.

📊 A ₹5 lakh loan at 12% interest = You pay back ₹8.9 lakh. That's ₹3.9 lakh STOLEN from your future.

🔓 Interest-Free Living = True Financial Freedom.

How to Live Interest-Free:

  • ✅ Save first, buy later — patience builds wealth
  • ✅ Emergency fund = 6 months expenses (no need for loans)
  • ✅ Use debit card, avoid credit cards completely
  • ✅ Buy what you can afford, not what you desire
  • ✅ Community lending circles (interest-free mutual help)
  • ✅ Negotiate cash discounts instead of EMI
